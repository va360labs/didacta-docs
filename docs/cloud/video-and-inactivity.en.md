# Video, inactivity and backups

Didacta Cloud is free, and to keep it free and stable there are reasonable limits on video and a policy for abandoned academies. Here are the numbers.

## Uploading video

In a **Video** lesson you can **upload the file** from your computer or phone, or **paste a link** (YouTube, Vimeo…). Links to videos hosted outside Didacta are never touched.

For the videos you upload to Didacta:

| | Before your first sale | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stored video** | 5 hours | 50 hours, plus 10 hours for every €30 of Didacta fee in the last 12 months |
| **Maximum quality** | 720p | 1080p |
| **Hours watched per month** | 100 hours | No cap |
| **Per file** | 4 GB and 2 hours | 4 GB and 2 hours |

- "First sale" counts if you have sold something and have not fully refunded it.
- Limits are checked **before uploading**: if a file does not fit, the academy tells you without a single byte being sent, and shows how much you would have after your first sale. When the video finishes processing it is checked again with its real duration.
- If your academy does not have video upload available, the lesson tells you and you can still add the video by pasting a link.

## Academies with no activity

To keep Didacta Cloud free, abandoned academies are not kept indefinitely. The following count as **activity**:

- you sign in to your academy;
- a student signs in or watches something;
- there is a sale;
- there is a live subscription from any student.

| No activity for… | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **10 days** | It warns you by email. |
| **20 days** | It warns you again. |
| **30 days** | **The videos you uploaded to Didacta are deleted** and your academy is **archived**. |

- **Archiving is not deleting.** The academy stops being open, but the rest of its content, your students and your sales history are kept. To reopen it, write to [hola@didacta.io](mailto:hola@didacta.io).
- **Any new activity restarts the countdown.**
- At least 10 days pass between one warning and the next, and between the last warning and the deletion, and no video is deleted without a **confirmed backup**.
- Videos hosted outside Didacta (YouTube, Vimeo or your own account with another provider) are not touched.

!!! tip "How to avoid it"
    Just sign in to your academy now and then. If you are really going to leave it idle, [ask for a copy of your data](data-and-migration.md) first.

## Backups

- Didacta makes **encrypted backups of the service every night**. It keeps those of the **last 30 days** and **one per month for 12 months**; after that they are deleted automatically.
- **Before deleting your videos for inactivity**, Didacta makes a separate copy that it keeps for **90 days**. Within that period you can ask for them to be recovered; after it, the copy is permanently deleted.
- If you ask for **personal data to be erased** (yours or, as controller, your students'), the erasure also reaches those copies.
- Records of the sales collected on your behalf, of the fees and of the transfers are kept for **6 years**, as commercial and tax law requires.

The nightly backups are there so Didacta can restore the service; they are not a "download my academy" button. To take your data with you, see [Leaving and moving your data](data-and-migration.md).

# Support

Didacta Cloud support is provided by a small team **by email**. There are no guaranteed response times and no service-level agreement: it responds as soon as it can.

## Who to write to

| For… | Write to / use |
| --- | --- |
| A question, a problem with your academy, your payouts, your account, a copy of your data, reopening an archived academy or leaving | [hola@didacta.io](mailto:hola@didacta.io) (in Spanish or English) |
| A problem or an idea **from inside the academy** | The **Help & feedback** button → *Write to the Didacta team* (in simple mode, also **Get help** in "Start here") |
| A **security vulnerability** | [security@didacta.io](mailto:security@didacta.io), **never** a public issue. See the [security policy](https://didacta.io/en/security) |
| Commercial use or Enterprise licences | [licensing@didacta.io](mailto:licensing@didacta.io) |
| **Unlawful content** in an academy | [hola@didacta.io](mailto:hola@didacta.io): say why you think it is unlawful, the exact address (URL) and your name and email. A person reviews it and tells you what is decided and how to appeal. |

## The "Help & feedback" button

It is in the academy and lets you report something without leaving it:

1. Choose **I have a suggestion**, **Something is not working** or **I have a problem**.
2. Write what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. You can attach a **screenshot** (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB). The page, the browser and the time are sent too.
3. Follow the ticket in **Your tickets**: you get a notification when its status changes (new, in progress, resolved or dismissed). The Didacta team replies by email.

Tickets written by **your students** do not go to Didacta: they arrive in **your academy's** inbox, and you handle them (with their status and an internal note). You can switch that help off for your students from **Modules**.

## What to include to get help sooner

- Your academy's address (`<your-academy>.didacta.io`).
- What you were doing and what you expected to happen.
- A screenshot, if it is something visible.
- If it is a student's **dispute**, the evidence that they received the course (access logs, messages, an invoice): Didacta presents it to Stripe on your behalf.

## When the team gets into your academy

If the problem is only visible inside your academy, the Didacta team can open a **support access**. You do not need to give it your password. These are the rules:

- It lasts **at most 15 minutes**, is **single-use** and is opened with a **written reason**.
- It does not sign in *as you*: it opens a session as its own visible user, **"Soporte técnico (acceso temporal)"** (the name is shown in Spanish), with the academy administrator role.
- While it lasts, the academy shows a **banner that cannot be closed**: "Support access active. Someone from support is inside this space. Reason: …".
- It is **recorded in your academy's audit log**, and also under the name of whoever requested it on Didacta's side. That way both parties have the same story written separately.

## Decisions about your academy

If Didacta removes content or restricts your academy or your payouts, it sends you an explanation with the facts, the reason and the rule or section of the terms it is based on, and how to appeal. You can ask for a review during the following **6 months** by replying to the same email; it is reviewed by a person other than the one who decided, where possible in a small team. You can also go to court. Details in the [terms of use for creators](https://didacta.io/en/legal/terms) (sections 12 and 13).

# Invite and earn

"Invite and earn" lets **your students sell for you**: each one has a personal link and, when someone joins through it and pays, the person who invited them earns a percentage of what they pay. It is the [mod.referrals](../modules/catalog/referrals.md) module; here is how it is used on Didacta Cloud, in simple mode.

## Turning it on

The programme **starts off**. To turn it on:

1. Go to **Sales** (or click **Turn on referrals** in the idea "Start here" suggests once you are selling).
2. In the **Your students sell for you** block, click **Turn on referrals**. Then, in the same block, adjust **what the person who invites gets (%)**, from 0 to 100, and click **Save**. The default is 30%.
3. Once you have a course or a membership on sale, each student will see **Invite and earn** in their avatar menu, with their link.

You can turn it off whenever you like, and change the percentage: the change only affects what is earned from then on; each commission keeps the percentage it was created with.

## How the student earns

| Rule | Default |
| --- | --- |
| The person stays tied to whoever invited them from the moment they open the link for… | 30 days |
| What is paid | The percentage of what that person **actually pays**: each course purchase and each membership payment. Free trials and €0 payments generate no commission. |
| The commission stays pending… | 14 days from the payment; after that, confirmed. |
| Refunds | If the sale is refunded in full before it is paid out, the commission is cancelled; if part of it is refunded, it is reduced proportionally. |
| Self-referral | Your own link does not count for you, not even with another account that has the same email. |

Only the first valid link the person opens counts. Of the people they invite, the student only sees their initials, the date they joined and what they have generated.

## How the student gets paid

They are paid **by you, not by Didacta**: the one who pays them is your academy, and Didacta only provides the tool (it withholds nothing and is not involved in the payment). In one of two ways:

- **As a discount on their fee**, if you have the **Let them use it as a discount on their fee** option on and the student has an active membership. It subtracts what they earned from their next fee, up to 80% of one fee (so each fee keeps a real charge). You cover it: that fee reaches you with the discount. If the student cancels with an unused discount, that balance goes back to their wallet to be taken as cash.
- **In cash.** The student requests to be paid what they earned, giving their name, tax ID and IBAN (stored encrypted). You see the request in **Payment requests**, make the **bank transfer** and mark it as paid with the **transfer reference**: a PDF receipt with a consecutive number is generated, which both of you can download. There may be a minimum to request it (€50 by default).

## Terms and obligations

- Before getting their link, the student must be **over 18** and accept the programme's terms (at `/referral-terms`, with your academy as the party). The date and version they accepted are stored.
- If a sale whose commission was **already paid** is refunded, a **refund adjustment** is created and subtracted from what the student earns next; they are never asked for money back.
- **Each party answers for its own taxes.** What the student receives is paid by your academy, not by Didacta: if the law requires you to withhold or report it, you do it. Didacta does not withhold, does not file returns on anyone's behalf and does not give tax advice.
- Didacta's 5% is calculated, as always, on what each buyer actually pays.

All the advanced options (guarantee period, attribution window, minimum, requiring the referrer to hold a membership, settlements, revoking a commission…) are in [mod.referrals](../modules/catalog/referrals.md), in full mode.

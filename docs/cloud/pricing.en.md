# Pricing and fee

Didacta Cloud has **a single plan and everything is included**. There are no tiers and no paid extras, and the price does not depend on how many students you have.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly fee** | 0 |
| **Signing up** | 0 |
| **When you sell** | 5% of each sale, taxes included |
| **Card fee** | Separate. Charged by Stripe, the payment processor |

If you do not sell in a month, you pay nothing. There is no trial that runs out, and you do not need a card to sign up.

## Which sales carry the 5%

Every sale you make to your students through Didacta Cloud: single courses, **every membership payment** (renewals included) and any other product you sell in your academy.

- The 5% is calculated on **what the student pays**, taxes included if there are any, in the currency Stripe settles the payment in (the currency of your payout account).
- It is rounded to the nearest cent; halves round up.

## What you get: two examples

For a creator in Spain whose student pays with a standard European card:

| Sale | Card (Stripe) | Didacta (5%) | You get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| €30 | €0.70 | €1.50 | **€27.80** |
| €100 | €1.75 | €5.00 | **€93.25** |

Cards from outside Europe cost more: on a €100 sale paid with a US card, the card fee is €3.40 and you get €91.60. For creators based outside Spain the card fee can be different.

!!! note "The estimate when you set a price is approximate"
    When you set a price, the academy shows you something like "with €49 you get ~€44". It is an estimate using the standard card rate. What is actually deducted is the real fee of each payment, which you can see broken down on every sale (**Sales → See details**).

## Card fee

Stripe charges it on every payment; it is separate from the 5% and you pay it. For a creator in Spain with a standard European card it is currently **1.5% + €0.25**.

- If you set the price in a currency other than your payout account's, Stripe adds a conversion cost that is part of that fee. The price screen warns you about it.
- Other Stripe costs that are not charged on each payment (maintaining your payout account, each transfer to your bank, subscription handling) are borne by Didacta today and are not passed on to you.

## Taxes on the fee

The 5% **includes taxes**: you pay 5% and nothing more, wherever you are. What changes with your country is how it is itemised on the invoice:

| Your situation | How it appears on the invoice |
| --- | --- |
| Account in Spain | The 5% includes 21% VAT. On a €30 sale, Didacta's €1.50 is €1.24 net plus €0.26 VAT; you still receive €27.80. |
| Company in another EU country with a valid intra-EU VAT number | Invoice without VAT, which you account for yourself (reverse charge). |
| Outside the EU | The fee is not subject to Spanish VAT; you may have to declare some tax on it in your country. |

Every month you receive an **invoice for the previous month's fees** (the first one arrives at the start of the month after your first sale), which you can download as a PDF from your panel. You do not have to pay it: it was already deducted from your sales. To issue it, your [tax details](refunds-and-taxes.md#tax-details) are needed.

## Founder rate

If you are one of the **first 100 creators** to sign up, your 5% is **guaranteed for 24 months from your sign-up**, even if the general price changes. The order is counted by the sign-up date and time the system records, and a place is not reused if someone leaves.

## Price changes

If Didacta changes the fee or the way it is calculated, it tells you by email **at least 30 days in advance**. The change only affects sales after it takes effect and, if you disagree, you can end the contract beforehand at no cost.

## Access granted by hand

You can give access to your academy without the student paying through Didacta in specific cases: students who already bought from you elsewhere, scholarships, invitations. What is not allowed is using it as a regular way to sell outside Didacta (by bank transfer, in cash or through another gateway) to avoid the fee.

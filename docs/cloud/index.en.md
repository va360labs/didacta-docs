# Didacta Cloud

**Didacta Cloud** is the managed edition: VA360 LABS hosts and maintains your academy, and you just teach and sell. It is **free**: there is no sign-up fee and no monthly fee. Didacta keeps **5% of every sale** you make to your students, and the card fee (Stripe) is separate.

Opening an academy takes a few minutes and needs no card: [didacta.io/en/start](https://didacta.io/en/start).

## How it works

- **The same product as Community.** Cloud runs the published Didacta image, the same one a Community self-hoster downloads: there is no "Cloud-only" version of the teaching features. VA360 LABS applies the updates; you install and upgrade nothing.
- **Your academy has its own address** (`https://<your-academy-name>.didacta.io`) and its data is isolated from every other academy's. Your students' data is stored on servers in the EU.
- **It starts in simple mode.** A short menu across the top and one screen, "Start here", that takes you from zero to your first sale. Whenever you want, you can switch to the [full mode](../installation/visual-walkthrough.md#10-simple-mode).
- **You are the seller.** You set the price and the terms; on your student's receipt and bank statement, the seller is you. Didacta collects the payment on your behalf with Stripe, keeps its fee and transfers the rest to you every week. Details in [Payouts](payouts.md).
- **Languages:** the academy can run in Spanish, English or Brazilian Portuguese.

## What is included

Everything the product offers, with no tiers and no paid extras: courses with lessons, quizzes and exams, certificates, community, messages, live classes with your video-call link (or with Zoom connected), memberships and single-course sales, the "Invite and earn" programme and the automated retention messages. Every module is described in the [module catalog](../modules/catalog/index.md).

!!! note "What is not included today"
    A custom domain, a mobile app, and support with guaranteed response times or a service-level agreement. The full list of limits is in [Limits and differences from Community](cloud-vs-community.md).
